    How your history becomes music
    Each commit
    is one note, played by its author's instrument.
    Lines added
    push the melody up. Lines removed pull it down.
    Big commits
    last longer and get an ornament.
    Quiet weeks
    become rests.
    Fixes
    hit the rim of the darbuka. Merges hit its centre. Reverts shake the riq and undo the last melodic step.
    Phrases
    come home to the tonic or the ghammaz every eight notes.
    The opening
    is a free-rhythm taqsim on the oud, made from your first commits.
    The maqam
    is chosen from the character of the repo, and the tempo from how busy it is.
